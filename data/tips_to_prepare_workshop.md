### Tips to Prepare a Workshop

I co-organized the ICRA 2025 workshop on contact-rich manipulation. We received 40 submissions and had over 300 in-person attendees. Here is what I learned.

#### 1. Proposal

- **Start early.** Proposal deadlines are months before the conference; we started in August 2024 for a May 2025 workshop.
- **Pick a topic that is timely, relevant, and well-scoped.**
- **Ask senior professors to send speaker invitations.** Emails from the organizers' advisors get faster and more positive replies.

The ICRA decision came at the end of December. Use the quiet wait to outline logistics and the speaker lineup.

#### 2. Planning

- **Confirm speaker availability first.** Many speakers attend only part of the conference.
- **Keep sponsorship needs minimal.** Companies that sponsored the conference before are the best ones to ask, but the less you depend on them, the simpler planning is.
- **Track the numbers:** expected attendance, paper submissions, and your assigned day or half-day. Speakers will ask.
- **Keep the website current.** It is often the only way outside attendees find you. List speakers, affiliations, and the research themes. (Thanks to Wei-Cheng for our excellent website!)

#### 3. Workshop Papers

Encourage early results, position papers, and system descriptions. Preliminary work in an informal setting is what starts discussions and collaborations.

#### 4. On Site

- **Get local help if you can.** (Thanks to Yunhai Han for transporting our food and drinks!)
- **Zoom:** log in early and test screen sharing.
- **Microphones:** have a backup plan for feedback or audio failure.
- **Lighting:** rooms can be too dim or too bright; check ahead of time.
