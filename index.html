Shaoxiong Yao

I am a research scientist at Dyna, working on dexterous manipulation. I recently received my PhD in Computer Science from University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, where I was advised by Prof. Kris Hauser. I have dual undergraduate degrees in Computer Science from University of Michigan and Electrical and Computer Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

CV  /  Biography

Research Interests

My research focuses on developing physically grounded models for robotic manipulation through multimodal real-world observations, including vision, touch, and language, with works in Robotic Manipulation [CVPR 2026, ICRA 2026 Workshop, ICRA 2025], Multimodal Perception [ICRA 2023, CoRL 2024, ICRA 2025] and Physical Modeling [ICRA 2024].
